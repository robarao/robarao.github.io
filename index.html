Research

PhD Candidate, MPH

My dissertation develops a mathematical framework for detecting signal amid noise in high-dimensional clinical data — one that allows for the integration of multiple sources of data — with direct applications to patient retention and workflow optimization across the spectrum of early- to late-stage clinical research. It builds on 15+ years working the statistical side of clinical trials, from Phase I–III biostatistics at Daiichi Sankyo and Fred Hutch to a PhD Fellowship at the FDA studying biosimilar interchangeability — the throughline is turning messy, high-dimensional clinical data into decisions that hold up under regulatory and scientific scrutiny.
